Dear Editor,

We are pleased to submit our manuscript entitled **“Pilot-Scale Text Column Detection and Reading-Order Recovery for Traditional Mongolian Historical Archives”** for consideration in *Pattern Recognition Letters*.

This manuscript addresses a low-resource historical document analysis problem: page-level text-column detection and reading-order recovery for vertically written handwritten traditional Mongolian archival pages. The work is intentionally positioned as a concise pilot-scale layout-analysis benchmark rather than a complete OCR system. Because reliable expert transcription of the handwritten Mongolian columns is not yet available, we do not report CER/WER; instead, we provide a rigorously evaluated crop-export-ready interface that can support future OCR evaluation once expert transcripts are added.

The main contributions are: (1) a page-level annotation protocol for traditional Mongolian archive pages, including text-column boxes, ignore regions, orientation metadata, and reading order; (2) a unified benchmark comparing heuristic extraction, YOLOv8n, and a COCO-pretrained Faster R-CNN MobileNet detector; and (3) an explicit discussion of strict page-level limitations, including the fact that the current system is best viewed as a candidate crop generator rather than a fully automatic page-level OCR preprocessing solution.

We believe the manuscript is suitable for *Pattern Recognition Letters* because it is concise, focused, and reports a practical pattern-recognition problem in an underrepresented script and archival setting. The study is also deliberately cautious in its claims: it separates layout evaluation from future transcription-based OCR evaluation and makes the non-image artifacts reproducible subject to archive-image access restrictions.

This manuscript has not been published and is not under consideration elsewhere. All authors have approved the submission.

Sincerely,
