# Highlights

- A pilot-scale benchmark is introduced for traditional Mongolian historical archive page layout analysis.
- The annotation protocol covers vertical text-column boxes, Ignore regions, scan orientation, and reading order.
- YOLOv8n and Faster R-CNN are compared with heuristic and diagnostic transfer baselines under unified evaluation.
- Evaluation includes TP/FP/FN counts, IoU@0.75, AP, bootstrap confidence intervals, runtime, and IAA.
- A crop-export-ready interface and expert-transcription package are prepared for future OCR case studies.
