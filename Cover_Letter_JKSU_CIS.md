Dear Editor,

We submit our manuscript entitled **“A Low-Resource Benchmark for Layout-Aware Text Column Detection and Reading-Order Recovery in Traditional Mongolian Historical Archives”** for consideration in the *Journal of King Saud University Computer and Information Sciences*.

This work addresses a low-resource historical document analysis problem: page-level text-column detection and reading-order recovery for vertically written handwritten traditional Mongolian archival pages. The manuscript is intentionally positioned as a full application-oriented layout-analysis study rather than a complete OCR system. Because reliable expert transcription of the handwritten Mongolian columns is not yet available, we do not report CER/WER; instead, we provide a rigorously evaluated crop-export-ready interface that supports future OCR evaluation once expert transcripts become available.

The manuscript contributes: (1) a page-level annotation protocol for traditional Mongolian archive pages, including text-column boxes, ignore regions, orientation metadata, and reading order; (2) a unified comparison of heuristic extraction, YOLOv8n, and a COCO-pretrained Faster R-CNN MobileNet detector; and (3) a transparent discussion of strict page-level limitations, including the fact that the current system should be viewed as a candidate crop generator rather than a fully automatic page-level OCR preprocessing solution.

We believe the manuscript fits the journal’s scope in computer vision, pattern recognition, image processing, and machine learning, while also offering a culturally significant and underrepresented archival document analysis problem. This manuscript is original, has not been published elsewhere, and is not under consideration by another journal.

Sincerely,

Lanying Liang and Yuefeng Liu
